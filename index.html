SETPACK

A 2D lighting, shoot planning and set-marking tool for iPad. Built for the film industry.

  • Place lights, camera, grip and set dressing on a plan. Everything starts with a point.
  • Import a floor plan. Export a PDF the crew can actually read on set.
  • Runs entirely on your iPad. No account, no tracking, no servers.

SETPACK is in private beta. We are inviting a small number of working film crew to use it on real jobs and tell us what breaks.
